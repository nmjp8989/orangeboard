Your Renovation Questions, Answered.

Do I need a permit for my renovation?

It depends on the scope of work and the municipality. We’ll review your proposed changes and help coordinate the applicable permit process. Confirm requirements with your local building department before work begins.

How long does a typical renovation take?

The timeline depends on the scope, existing conditions, material lead times, permit approvals and trade availability. We discuss the expected sequence and schedule during planning.

How much will my renovation cost?

Costs vary with the size of the space, design, materials, structural changes and existing conditions. A consultation helps establish a realistic scope and budget before detailed pricing.

Do you provide design and construction drawings?

We coordinate design and drawing requirements for your project. Specialized work may require appropriately qualified design or engineering professionals.

Can you help with city permits and inspections?

Yes, we can help coordinate applicable municipal requirements, applications and inspection scheduling. The municipality determines approval and inspection requirements.

Do you manage all the trades?

We coordinate the project and the trades required for the agreed scope. The exact responsibilities are discussed during project planning.

Can I live in my home during the renovation?

Depending on the scope, it may be possible to stay in the home. We discuss access, utilities, noise, dust and safety so you can plan suitable living arrangements.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Toronto, Mississauga, Brampton, Vaughan, Etobicoke, Oakville, Burlington, Richmond Hill, Markham and North York. See our Service Areas page.

How do I get a quote or start a project?

Book a free consultation or call us at 416 839 2265. Tell us about your goals, location, approximate budget and preferred timeline.
