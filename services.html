Helpful answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a permit?

Some structural, plumbing and other alterations require permits. Requirements depend on the work and municipality.

How long will my renovation take?

Timing depends on the scope, material lead times, permits and site conditions. A project-specific schedule is provided during planning.

Can you work with my budget?

Share your target budget so scope and material options can be discussed before a detailed estimate.

Can I live at home during construction?

This depends on the project scope, access to essential rooms and site safety.
