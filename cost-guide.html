Questions to Ask Before You Begin

  • Will the layout or structure change?
  • What materials and finish levels do you prefer?
  • Will electrical, plumbing or HVAC work be required?
  • Are permits, design drawings or engineering needed?
  • What contingency is appropriate for hidden conditions?
