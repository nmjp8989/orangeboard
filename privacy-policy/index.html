Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal information.

At Craftwell, we aim to protect the personal information you choose to share with us. This notice describes the current website’s consultation and information-handling features.

1. Information We Collect

If you contact us, you may provide your name, telephone number, email address, postal code, project details and any photos or drawings you choose to attach to your email. The consultation form on this website prepares an email draft; it does not upload files or submit information to a website database.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information you choose to send may be used to respond to inquiries, discuss consultations and estimates, coordinate a project and communicate relevant project updates.

3. How We Protect Your Information

Craftwell aims to handle personal information responsibly and limit access to those who need it. Email and hosting services have their own security and privacy practices.

4. Sharing Your Information

Information may need to be shared with appropriate project partners to deliver requested services, with your authorization or where required by law.

5. Cookies and Website Tracking

The site’s custom code does not set tracking cookies or use analytics. The website host and any email provider may process technical information under their own privacy practices.

6. Your Choices

Contact us to ask about your personal information or request access, correction or deletion, subject to applicable requirements.

7. Changes to This Policy

This page may be updated as our website and information-handling practices change. Please contact us with any questions.
